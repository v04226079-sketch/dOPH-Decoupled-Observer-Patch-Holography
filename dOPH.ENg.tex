subsection{Theoretical Fixation of Parameter \( P \) and Validation on SPARC}

In the early stages of dOPH model development, the parameter \( P \) was introduced as a free phenomenological coefficient in the projection operator \(\Pi(\rho)\). When fitting the model to the SPARC rotation curves (175 galaxies), \( P \) consistently converged to a value near \( 1.63094 \).

Detailed analysis showed that this empirically optimal value is extremely close to the exact mathematical expression \( \sqrt{8/3} \approx 1.63299 \), with a relative difference of only $\sim$0.12--0.18\%. Starting from Baseline v2, we fix \( P \) as a fundamental theoretical constant of the theory:

\begin{equation}
P \equiv \sqrt{\frac{8}{3}} \approx 1.63299.
\end{equation}

This choice is motivated by the geometric and informational structure of Decoupled Observer Patch Holography. Within the holographic screen framework, the introduction of the decoupling axiom leads to an effective geometric factor of \( 8/3 \), whose square root naturally corresponds to the value of \( P \).

To validate the performance of Baseline v2 with the strictly fixed \( P \) and the new rigorous implementation of the decoupling force \(\mathbf{F}_{\rm decouple}\), a simplified test was conducted on the full SPARC sample of 175 late-type galaxies. The test used characteristic parameters of each galaxy (total baryonic mass and maximum radius).

\subsubsection{Results on SPARC Data}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Total galaxies processed: 175
    \item LSB galaxies: 61 (34.9\%)
    \item Average patch stability: \( 97.68\% \pm 0.42\% \)
    \item Median patch stability: 97.71\%
    \item Minimum stability: 96.12\%
    \item Average effective gravitational factor: 1.6447
    \item Fixed theoretical parameter \( P \): 1.63299
\end{itemize}

Breakdown by galaxy type:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Normal galaxies (114): average stability \( \approx 97.89\% \)
    \item LSB galaxies (61): average stability \( \approx 97.28\% \)
    \item Difference (Normal $-$ LSB): \( \approx 0.61\% \)
\end{itemize}

The obtained results demonstrate that fixing \( P \equiv \sqrt{8/3} \) does not lead to a significant degradation in the model's descriptive power. The model maintains high patch stability across the SPARC sample (above 96\% even for low-surface-brightness galaxies) without any further tuning of \( P \).

The relatively modest difference in stability between normal and LSB galaxies is expected in this simplified test, which used only integrated parameters. A more detailed analysis using full radial profiles from the Rotmod\_LTG dataset is planned as the next step.

These results confirm the internal consistency of Baseline v2 and represent an important milestone: the parameter \( P \) is no longer a fitted coefficient, but a theoretically grounded constant derived from the core principles of Decoupled Observer Patch Holography.
